# Hobbies

Sport has been an important part of my life alongside study and work. Through rugby team captaincy and sports coaching, I have developed leadership, communication and the ability to support people with different needs.

I hold a Level 2 Certificate in Coaching (Sport and Physical Activity) and have delivered inclusive sports sessions for young people with autism, adapting activities to individual needs.

## Skills developed

- **Leadership:** Supported teammates and communicated clearly through rugby team captaincy.
- **Inclusive communication:** Adapted coaching instructions and activities to help young people participate.
- **Teamwork:** Worked with others towards shared goals in sport and coaching.
