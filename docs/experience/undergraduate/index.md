# Undergraduate

I studied BSc (Hons) Biochemistry at the University of Leeds, graduating with a 2:1. Through taught modules, laboratory work and independent study, I developed a foundation in molecular biology and the biochemical processes that underpin health and disease.

My final-year dissertation was a literature review of monoclonal antibody aggregation, its implications for therapeutic development and manufacturing, and approaches to reducing aggregation.

## Skills developed

- **Critical evaluation:** Assessed published research and compared evidence through my dissertation and academic assignments.
- **Laboratory practice:** Developed practical scientific skills and an understanding of experimental design through undergraduate laboratory training.
- **Scientific communication:** Brought together research findings and explained biochemical concepts clearly in written work.
